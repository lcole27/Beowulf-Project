⚔️ Frequently Asked Questions ⚔️

Q: Do you really fight monsters for free?
A: Yes. Glory is my payment. The Booty Tip is optional but strongly encouraged.
Q: What do you mean by "booty tip"?
A: Get your mind out of the rain-pipe. I mean treasure, obviously. I'm partial to swords with names.
Q: Do you use weapons?
A: Usually no. My preferred weapon is raw strength. However, magic swords are acceptable in swamp emergencies.
Q: What monsters do you specialize in?
A: corpse-makers, swamp-hags, sea-beasts, dragons, politicians, and occasionally troublesome mead hall patrons.
Q: Do you accept international jobs?
A: Yes. Denmark, Geatland, Sweden, and most monster-infested territories.
Q: What happens if you die fighting a monster?
A: Then my name will live forever in song. Please leave a positive review.
Q: Why are you fighting this dragon instead of just giving the stuff the thief stole back?
A: This Dragon is insulting my name and my very honor by attacking Geatland. He simply must die, and if I die, then my name will live on forever.
Q: .....and what will happen to your people?
A: Since they tend to be cowards, I imagine they will be slaughtered by my many enemies. Pity, that.
